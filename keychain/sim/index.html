How it plays

  • Keys 1·2·3: your Gremlet runs to that spot and the fireflies swarm there, like the koi.
  • Turn the knob: steer it left or right; the fireflies circle.
  • Spin fast: it gets dizzy.
  • Press the knob: drop a berry. Double press: pet. Hold: menu.
  • Turn: a firefly circles the rim and your Gremlet chases it.
  • Spin fast: it gets dizzy.
  • Press: drop a berry where the firefly is (it walks over and eats).
  • Double press: pet it.
  • Hold: menu (Feed, Water moss, Play, Sleep, Clock). Turn to pick, press to choose.
  • At night it sleeps; a press wakes it.
